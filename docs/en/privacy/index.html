Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to your email address when you join the waiting list.

Controller

A Brazileira, Torres Vedras, Portugal. Contact: inn@abrazileira.com.

Data

Your email address, the language of the page and the date and time of your consent.

Purpose

To let you know when A Brazileira opens.

Legal basis

Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR). Giving your email address is optional; without it, we cannot let you know. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before. To do so, write to inn@abrazileira.com.

Retention

Until you withdraw your consent or, at most, 24 months after you sign up.

Processor

Supabase, which hosts the database on servers in the European Union (Paris). When it receives the form, it also keeps technical request data (IP address and browser) for a short time.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

Transfers

Supabase and GitHub are companies based in the United States. If they process data outside the European Union, they do so with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, through inn@abrazileira.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).

Cookies

This site does not use cookies or analytics tools. If you choose Day or Night, that choice is stored only in your browser. Apart from hosting, the only request to a third party happens when you submit the form.
